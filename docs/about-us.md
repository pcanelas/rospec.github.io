# About rospec

## Authors

This is a research project within the [Software and Societal Systems Department (S3D)](https://s3d.cmu.edu/) and the [Robotics Institute](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/) at Carnegie Mellon University, and [LASIGE](https://lasige.pt/) at University of Lisbon, by:
- [Paulo Canelas](https://pcanelas.com/), with Carnegie Mellon University and University of Lisbon;
- [Bradley Schmerl](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~schmerl/), with Carnegie Mellon University;
- [Alcides Fonseca](https://wiki.alcidesfonseca.com/), with University of Lisbon;
- [Christopher S. Timperley](https://chris.timperley.info/), with Carnegie Mellon University.

## Code 
This project is open-source and available on [GitHub](https://github.com/pcanelas/rospec).
